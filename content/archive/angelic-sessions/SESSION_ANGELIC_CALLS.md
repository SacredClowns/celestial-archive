# The Angelic Calls

## April–July 1584 — Kraków

---

## Historical Context

The nineteen Angelic Calls (often called Keys) are the linguistic core of the Enochian system. ◆ They were received in reverse order: Call 19 first, Call 1 last. Kelley dictated Enochian text backward; English translations were given separately and aligned afterward.

This reception pattern is unusual and consequential. It means the correspondence between Enochian and English was established after the fact — a central problem for linguistic analysis and a central feature of the system's mystery.

---

## What the Record Says

The Calls describe purposes: opening the gates, invoking elemental forces, ascending through the Aethyrs. Call 19 is unique: thirty variants, each inserting an Aethyr name into a fixed position in the text. ◆ The Language Chamber's Call viewer presents Dee's primary text alongside the Aethyr insertions.

Peterson's critical edition remains the standard witness for manuscript readings. Laycock's dictionary analysis treats the Calls as the main corpus for attested Enochian vocabulary.

---

## What Was Received

- **Nineteen Calls** in Enochian with English translations
- **Reverse reception order** (19 → 1)
- **Call 19 variants** for the thirty Aethyrs
- **Vocabulary** that feeds the 631-word dictionary in this Archive
