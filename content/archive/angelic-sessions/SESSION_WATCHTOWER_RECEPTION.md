# The Watchtower Reception

## June–July 1584 — Kraków

---

## Historical Context

The four Watchtower tablets and the Tablet of Union form the spatial architecture of the Enochian cosmos. ◆ Received letter by letter during sessions at Kraków, they are among the most studied and most rearranged elements of the corpus. Dee's original arrangement differs from the Golden Dawn's elemental assignments — a difference the Archive marks, not resolves.

---

## What the Record Says

Each tablet is a grid of letters from which angel names are extracted by specific reading rules: Seniors along crosses, Kings in particular positions, lesser angels in sub-quadrants. ◆ The Tablet of Union binds the four elemental quarters with a fifth element: Spirit.

The 1587 "reformed" table — transmitted after the cross-matching incident — complicates the manuscript tradition. Multiple states of the Great Table exist in scholarship and in practice.

---

## What Was Received

- **Four Watchtower tablets** (Air, Water, Earth, Fire in Dee's assignment)
- **The Great Table** as combined master grid
- **The Tablet of Union** (4×5 letters)
- **Derivation rules** for hundreds of angel names
- **The 30 Aethyrs and 91 Governors** (described in related sessions)
