## Central Theme

Aethyrs are the system's strongest **collapse point** — toward literal cosmology, pure psychology, or ritual technology. The Observer maps readings without traveling as initiate.

**Signature question:** *What stays constant when different walkers describe the same room differently?*

## Learning Goals

1. Separate Dee-era Aethyr structure (names, governors, Call 19 key) from later journey practices.
2. Compare two practitioner accounts of one Aethyr — constants vs variables.
3. Explain why Aethyrs resist single-framework closure more than Watchtowers or Heptarchia.
4. **Preview** Comparative Discernment (full lesson 3.5) — four moves applied to one Aethyr parallel claim.
