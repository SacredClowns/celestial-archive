## Central Theme

Sessions are not a pipeline (angel → Kelley → Dee). They are **negotiation**: questions, resistance, repetition, revision, breakdown.

**Signature question:** *What happens in the room when the vision is unclear — and who shapes the answer?*

## Learning Goals

1. Describe session dynamics at process granularity (question, vision, report, record, correction).
2. Identify three+ diary moments of friction (Kelley refuses, Dee pushes back, material dictated twice differently).
3. Explain how Dee's participation complicates Single-Witness Discernment → **two-witness interdependence**.
4. Distinguish transcript reading ("they said X") from interaction reading ("they negotiated X").

## Structural Outline

### Opening
A session mid-stream: Kelley reports something incomplete; Dee asks again. No melodrama — documentary tension.

### Core I — The Room Restored
Holy Table, Sigillum, shew-stone (**◆** BM SLCups.232), posture, prayer. Link to Lesson 1.2 but with **process** emphasis.

### Core II — Curated Process Passages
- Kelley resistance / distress (**◆**)
- Dee verification request (**◆**)
- Contradiction or revision within sessions (**◆**)
- Domestic interruption (texture)

Each passage: **Interaction map** sidebar — who initiates, who redirects, who records.

### Core III — From Single Witness to Dyad
Teach without pathologizing:
- Kelley sole percipient of "angelic" content
- Dee sole author of surviving text
- Neither independent of the other's framing

**Cue Single-Witness Discernment** (Lesson 1.3) — recognized, not reintroduced.

### Core IV — Occult vs Documentary "Medium"
**○** channel/recorder model (Golden Dawn, spiritualist lineages) — attributed.
**◆** what diaries show about questions and answers.
**△ Psychological:** collaborative creativity, folie à deux as *framework* not diagnosis — limits named.

### Lens Sections

### Discernment Box
**Two-witness grid extension:** sincerity × accuracy now applies to *relationship*, not only Kelley.
