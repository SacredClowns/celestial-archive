## 6. Cross-Rank Patterns

**Are ◇ badges over-represented?**
Slightly, but justifiably. The Student rank draws heavily on Laycock (1978/2001), Asprem (2012), Peterson (2003), Clulee (1988), and Szonyi (2004). These are the standard scholarly references for this material. Most ◇ claims cite at least one of these by name. The ◇ density reflects the genuine state of scholarship — there is a body of consensus work, and the lessons draw on it. No action needed.

**Are ○ badges under-represented?**
Slightly. The Traditional Occult Lens sections in each lesson use ○ correctly, but many practitioner claims in the main body that are presented within the Traditional Occult Lens paragraphs could be more explicitly ○-marked. The current usage is defensible — the lens sections are clearly framed as presenting practitioner perspectives — but a future pass could add inline ○ markers to specific practitioner claims within the lens sections that currently rely on the section heading to provide the badge context. Not urgent.

**Is there a pattern of ◆ claims that are really ◇?**
One instance corrected: Mathers' transcriptions (2.5) were badged ◆ but are ◇ (scholarly inference, not manuscript attestation). Otherwise the ◆ usage is disciplined — the lessons consistently anchor ◆ claims to named manuscripts (Sloane MS 3188, 3189, 3191; Cotton Appendix XLVI).

**Any lesson that stands out as an outlier?**
Lesson 2.3 was the outlier. Its Symbolic Lens contained seven ◎ badges across two parallel claims (invocations, reverse-dictation) that did not meet the four qualification criteria. Both were typological resemblances presented with the authority of documented, specific parallels. This was the most significant badge inflation in the rank. Corrected.

## 7. Overall Badge Discipline Verdict

Student rank is badge-disciplined enough for controlled preview, with the corrections applied in this pass. The core epistemic machinery of the rank — the six seductions, the three discernment practices, the ◆/△ distinction between Dee's manuscripts and later additions — is well-executed and consistently badged. The primary systemic issue was ◎ overreach in the Symbolic Lens sections of Lessons 2.3 and 2.4, where typological resemblances (invocations across traditions, four-element schemes, mandala parallels) were given the weight of specific, documented, tradition-specific parallels. These have been corrected. After correction, the ◎ badge is used only where all four qualification criteria are met: the Heptarchia's Solomonic and Neoplatonic parallels (2.1), the sealed-book motif (2.2), the 49-correspondence (2.2), the Dionysian hierarchy (2.4), and the Neoplatonic progressive cosmology (2.6). A small number of stealth badges ("noteworthy," "intimately," "it is not nothing") were caught and softened. The rank's badge vocabulary is appropriately varied, with ◆, ◇, ○, △, ◎, ~, ?, and ⚠ all used in their correct registers. The lessons earn their claims. The rank is ready for human review.
