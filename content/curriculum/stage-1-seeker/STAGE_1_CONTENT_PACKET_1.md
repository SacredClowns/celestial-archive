## Central Theme

Introduce the Archive, the Enochian system as *documented question*, Dee and Kelley as historical actors, comparative "lost language" pattern, and epistemic badges (first encounter with ◆).

**Signature question:** *If there were a language before all languages, how would you know it when you heard it?*

## Learning Goals

1. Describe who Dee and Kelley were and why the partnership matters.
2. Explain what the system claims to be — and that the claim is debated.
3. Place Enochian in the cross-cultural "lost knowledge" pattern.
4. Distinguish evidence from interpretation using badges.
5. Hold ambiguity as starting position.
