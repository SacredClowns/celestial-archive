# Research Extraction: Geoffrey James — *The Enochian Magick of Dr. John Dee* (1984/1998)

---

## I. EXTRACTION THESIS

James presents the Enochian system in its original manuscript form, before any Golden Dawn modifications. His preface (pp. xiv–xxvii) offers an extended analysis of the Kelly authorship question, identifies specific Gnostic textual parallels with the Angelic Keys, and examines the linguistic characteristics of the Angelical language. Books One through Five then reproduce the system itself from Dee's manuscripts.

**Archive value:** This source provides (a) the complete word-by-word text of all 19 Calls with pronunciation and textual-critical notes, (b) a nuanced analysis of Kelly that goes beyond the simple fraud/genuine binary, (c) Gnostic parallels specific enough for our Symbolic Lens and comparative-systems research, and (d) the original Dee-manuscript form of the Watchtower system before Golden Dawn modifications.

## II. THE KELLY AUTHORSHIP QUESTION (Preface, pp. xv–xxv)

James identifies five possible explanations for Kelly's production of the Enochian material, refusing to collapse into any single verdict.

### The Five Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | James's Assessment | Archive Badge |
|---|---|---|
| **Kelly as conscious fraud** | Evidence does not justify this judgement. Kelly accepted £50/year from Dee, but this was standard Elizabethan patronage. Kelly repeatedly tried to leave Dee's employ and questioned the angelic nature of the spirits. ◇ | ? |
| **Kelly as unconscious plagiarist** | Plausible for some elements. Kelly participated in private magical ceremonies and may have had access to grimoire texts Dee didn't know about. Kelly brought Agrippa's *De Occulta Philosophia* to a session, and Dee was ignorant of books Kelly kept in his study. ◆ | ? |
| **Kelly as psychotic/dissociative** | Kelly exhibited signs of extreme stress and possible psychosis. He reported physical sensations of entities in his skull. Peter French suggests Dee may have propelled Kelly into artificial psychosis through the ceremonies. Kelly may have had multiple personalities — the spirits speak in biblical dialects quite different from Kelly's normal speech. ◇ | ? |
| **Kelly as genuine skryer** | The telekinetic phenomenon witnessed by Dee (stone rising and flying) and precognitive predictions (Spanish Armada, execution of Mary Queen of Scots) provide some evidence. However, spirits also predicted events that never occurred. ◇ | ? |
| **Kelly as complex combination** | James's own conclusion: "There is no simple explanation for Kelly's actions." He believed in the spirits, yet fabricated and plagiarized. He exhibited signs of psychosis, yet manifested extraordinary linguistic and telekinetic phenomena. ◇ | ? |

### Key Evidence Points

**Against simple fraud:**
- Stylistic differences between Kelly's normal writing and the spirit communications are pronounced ◇
- The complexity of the Angelical Keys argues against conscious fabrication — James compares it to Tolkien spending years on Elvish, while Kelly produced the keys in a matter of days ◇
- The first five keys were dictated letter by letter, backwards, without significant errors ◆
- The bulk of the keys (over 1000 words) were dictated on a single day ◆
- English glosses were also dictated on a single day, yet they match their Angelical counterparts almost perfectly ◆

**For possible plagiarism:**
- Elements of the Enochian system are similar to and in some cases almost identical to elements in other Renaissance magical texts ◇
- The *Arbatel* and the *Heptameron* are arranged like Book Two, with conjurations for rulers of each day ◇
- The Sigillum Dei Aemeth is based upon an earlier sigil in the *Sworn Book of Honorius* (Liber Juratus), a manuscript predating Kelly by almost 300 years ◆
- Methodology for devising spirit sigils from letter/number squares was described by Agrippa c. 1533 ◆
- Kelly brought Agrippa to a session; Dee was surprised Kelly had the book ◆
- Reginald Scot (1584) complained that conjurors carried books attributed to Enoch ◆

**Gnostic source hypothesis:**
- Overuse of the 'Z' phoneme is characteristic of Gnostic incantations ◇
- The Gnostic name for the demiurge, IALDABAOTH, is close to the Angelical IAD BALTOH ("God of Righteousness") ◇
- Both Gnostic and Angelical magic emphasize the number 49 ◇
- Both systems emphasize opening 'gates' into mystical realms — James quotes parallel passages from the *Book of the Great Logos* and from Kelly's scrying ◇

### The Heretical Doctrines (pp. xviii–xix)

The spirits communicated doctrines that were, in Elizabethan context, extremely dangerous heresies ◆:
- That Jesus was not God
- That no prayer ought to be made to Jesus
- That there is no sin
- That man's soul transmigrates (reincarnation)
- That the generation of mankind from Adam and Eve is not history but "a writing which has another sense"

Kelly was profoundly disturbed by these and his conversion back to orthodoxy indicates genuine remorse and guilt. ◆ Dee initially called the spirits "apparitions" and drew up an elaborate contract with the stipulation that these doctrines remain secret among the four participants (Dee, Kelly, and their two wives), on pain of death. ◆

**Archive relevance:** This material feeds directly into Lesson 1.5 ("The Long Arc and the Breaking") and the Breaking Problem framework. The doctrines are themselves a key exhibit for the Discernment Practices — particularly Single-Witness Discernment and Source Discernment.

### The Wife-Swapping Incident (pp. xxv–xxvi)

The spirits demanded that Dee and Kelly have sexual relations with each other's wives. ◆ Kelly was appalled and insisted on a written protest in Dee's magical diaries. ◆ Dee drew up the elaborate contract. The ceremonies continued, "possibly pushing Kelly beyond the limits of sanity." ◇

After splitting with Dee, Kelly claimed to have discovered the Philosopher's Stone, murdered a man in a manic rage, was imprisoned, and died falling (or jumping) from his tower prison. ◇

**Archive relevance:** Critical for the Breaking Problem framework. James's treatment provides a model for how to handle this material — he presents it without sensationalizing, notes Kelly's distress, and allows the reader to draw their own conclusions.

## III. LINGUISTIC ANALYSIS OF THE ANGELICAL (Preface, pp. xxii–xxiv)

### James's Argument: A Language Designed for Non-Human Speakers

James argues the Angelical language exhibits characteristics suggesting it was designed for non-spoken communication ◇:

1. **No onomatopoeia** — Human languages invariably contain sound-imitative words; Angelical has none ◇
2. **Conceptual contractions** — Words like TELOCVOVIM and JADOIASMOMA are contractions of concepts, not simplifications for easier pronunciation ◇ (e.g., TELOCVOVIM = TELOCH [death] + VOVIN [dragon] = "death-dragon," referring to Satan's transformation during his fall ◇)
3. **Unpronounceable consonant strings** — Many words feature strings of consonants that resist human vocalization ◇
4. **Random-seeming letter arrangements** — Angelical letter patterns appear to be more like a Cabbala than a spoken tongue ◇
5. **Incomprehensible number system** — Not based on 10 or any other known base ◇
6. **Hebrew as stated source language** — The language claims Hebrew as its source, connecting to Cabbalistic systems ◆

### Laycock vs. James on English Resemblance

James reports Laycock's claim that the Angelical is "English-like both in word order and in pronunciation" ◇ but then provides counterexamples:

- NIISO CRIP IP NIDALI is glossed as "Come away, but not your noyses" — but what it probably means is "leave wherever you are and come here, but don't make thundering noises" ◇
- TELOCVOVIM — much more Germanic than English-like ◇
- James argues: if the letter arrangement is random (as Laycock claims), then the English-like pronunciation cannot be an inherent quality — more likely Dee assigned English pronunciations because he wished to speak the keys in ceremony ◇

### Pronunciation Evidence

- Kelly, as the spirits' "mouthpiece," never pronounced the Angelical words ◆
- Kelly dictated letter by letter from a table he saw in the crystal ◆
- The angel "hath a rod or wand in his hand...it is of Gold...He standeth upon his round table of Christal...There appear an infinite number of letters on the same, as thick as can stand by another...He standeth and pointeth with his rod to the letters of his Table" ◆
- Dee assigned pronunciations to his native English phonology ◇

**Archive relevance:** This analysis enriches the Language Chamber's Pronunciation Guide section. It provides the scholarly basis for why we present three pronunciation traditions without ranking them. James's non-human-speaker hypothesis is a strong candidate for a Speculative Lens sidebar.

## IV. BOOK ONE: THE COSMOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK (pp. 1–16)

James presents the angels' own narrative of the Enochian system's origins, drawn from Dee's manuscripts. This is the mythological framework within which the entire system operates.

### Narrative Arc

1. **The Garden of Eden** — Adam knew all creation and spoke the Angelical language "which Adam verily spake in innocence" ◆
2. **The Origin of Hebrew** — After the Fall, Adam was "as dumb, and not able to speak." He learned a reduced version: Hebrew. But "the true forms and pronunciations are lost." ◆
3. **The Wisdom of Enoch** — God appeared to Enoch, who prayed thrice daily and was granted wisdom. After 50 days, Enoch wrote his books, titled: "LET THOSE THAT FEAR GOD AND ARE WORTHY — READ." ◆
4. **The Fall of Enoch's Magick** — The unworthy began to read the books. God sent 150 wicked spirits to counterfeit divine power. The memory of Enoch was washed away. ◆
5. **The Reestablishment** — God sends Raphael to Dee and Kelly to restore the Doctrine: "neither painted nor carved nor imagined by man — but simple, plain, full of strength and the power of the Holy Ghost." ◆
6. **The 49 Tables and Keys** — The system is contained in 49 Tables, with 49 voices/callings as Natural Keys to open 48 Gates of understanding (one Gate is not to be opened). ◆
7. **The Primæval Language** — The Angelical is presented as superior to Hebrew: "every letter signifieth the member of the substance whereof it speaketh. Every word signifieth the essence of the substance." ◆

### The Nine Powers of the Great Table

As stated by the angels, the Great Table of the Quarters contains ◆:
1. All human knowledge
2. Springeth Physic (medicine)
3. Knowledge of all elemental Creatures
4. Knowledge, finding, and use of metals, virtues of stones
5. Conjoining and destruction of all nature
6. Moving from place to place
7. Knowledge of all mechanical crafts
8. Transmutation of form, but not essence
9. Knowledge of the secrets of men

**Archive relevance:** This cosmological narrative is the framing material for the app's "Magick of Enoch" introductory pathway. The nine powers map directly to the angelic hierarchy functions in Book Five. The lingua adamica claim (Angelical > Hebrew > post-Babel languages) connects to Holtberg's analysis of Postel and the *prisca theologia* tradition.

### Textual-Critical Patterns Identified by James

James's footnotes reveal several systematic features:

1. **Consistent closing formula** — Calls 11–18 all end with the same sequence: ZORGE LAP ZIRDO NOCO MAD HOATH IAIDA ("Be friendly unto me for I am the servant of the same your God, the true worshipper of the Highest") ◇

2. **ZACAR/ZAMRAN command** — Appears in Calls 1, 2, 3, 7, 11–18, and 30: "Move, and shew yourselves; Open the Mysteries of your Creation" ◇

3. **Copying errors identified** — James flags specific errors in Sloane MS 3191 where the Royal Appendix (Cotton XLVI) preserves a better reading ◇:
   - 1.64: UL should be UL not LU (prefix reversal error)
   - 1.88: ZIR vs ZIRDO confusion
   - 4.21: CRIP for "but" (should be CRIP-L?)
   - 10.56: MATORB — English missing in Sl. MS 3191
   - 11.16–17: Enochian missing, English in margin

4. **Morphological analysis** — James identifies roots, contractions, and suffixes throughout:
   - TELOCVOVIM = TELOCH + VOVIN ("death" + "dragon") ◇
   - JADOIASMOMAR = IAD + OI + AS + MOMAR ("God" + "is" + "was" + "crowned") ◇
   - IADNAMAD = IAD + NAMAD ("God" + "knowledge") ◇
   - TOX may be a suffix signifying possession (see 13.21, 14.23) ◇
   - TILB may be "of her" (see 30.55, 30.63) ◇

5. **Cross-references** — James systematically notes when a word appears in multiple Calls, creating an implicit concordance. Key high-frequency words:
   - OD ("and") — appears in virtually every Call
   - DS ("which") — universal connector
   - CHIS ("are") — copula throughout
   - SOBA ("whose") — see 1.30, 1.60, 5.41, 6.23, 7.27, 9.14, etc.
   - BAGLE ("for/for why") — see 4.45, 5.61, 7.52, 8.49, 8.53, 9.65, etc.
   - CAOSGI/CAOSGO/CAOSG ("the earth/of the earth") — frequent

| Column | Content |

| 5 | Number of ministers |
| 6 | Total ministers per Order |
| 7 | Angelic King ruling |
| 8 | Tribe of Israel |
| 9 | Quarter of the Earth |

### Book Five: The Great Table (pp. 117–143)

James presents:
- **Original Great Table** (1583) — 25×27 letter grid ◆
- **Corrected Great Table** (April 20, 1587, "reformed by Raphael") ◆
- **Great Circle of the Quarters** — Diagram showing the 12 banners with Names of God from the four lines of the holy spirit ◆
- **Fundamental Obeisance** — Complete conjuration text invoking the 12 mystical Names ◆
- **Twenty-Four Seniors** — Names by quarter with God Names (BATAIVA/East, ICZHHCA/South, RAAGIOS/West, ELDPRNA/North) ◆
- **Angels of Medicine** — 16 good angels and 16 cacodemons, by quarter, with God Names and reversed God Names ◆
- **Angels of Precious Stones** — Same structure as Medicine ◆
- **Angels of Transformation** — Same structure ◆

Each category includes the complete invocation template text and the relevant sub-section of the Great Table diagram.

**Publication Verdict:** CLEARED

James's preface analysis is well-sourced and presents multiple perspectives without collapsing into a single verdict, which aligns with the Archive's epistemic framework. The Call texts are manuscript transcriptions with textual-critical apparatus. The hierarchy data is straightforward tabulation of primary source material.

**Caveats:**
- James's pronunciation column should be treated as one tradition among three (it broadly aligns with Laycock/Dee reconstruction but may contain James's own editorial choices)
- Some of James's Gnostic parallels are suggestive rather than conclusive — badge as ◇~ (scholarly-speculative) in curriculum use
- The book predates Peterson's comprehensive collation; any discrepancies should defer to Peterson for manuscript accuracy
