## "DONE" CRITERIA

The MVP is ready to launch when:

1. All items in "SHIPS IN MVP" are checked off
2. Content review is complete for all published pages (Human sign-off)
3. Discernment badges and source citations are present on 100% of content
4. Performance meets targets (< 2s load, 60fps interactions)
5. Accessibility audit passes (WCAG 2.1 AA)
6. 3 external testers have navigated the app and provided feedback
7. The app feels beautiful, not like a prototype
8. The human says: "Yes. This is the Celestial Archive."
