# Stage 2 Visual System

**Philosophy:**
The deeper room is not more true. It is only more complex.
Student surfaces must be emotionally flattened. They represent archival arrangement, not a puzzle box. The focus remains explicitly on surviving structures, intentional gaps, and transmission geometry without inserting hypothetical authority.

## Core Component Inventory

### 1. `LessonSafetyFrame`
- **Role**: Provides the critical epistemic boundary condition before structural comparisons are rendered. Assures the user how to read geometric displays (as arrangement, not truth).
- **Visual Priority**: Secondary. Rendered using a muted box format with low opacity borders to sink backwards from the primary prose.
- **Must Never Become**: A tutorial wizard, a "decoder instructions" panel, or an interruptive popup. 

### 2. `PendingComparativeArrangement`
- **Role**: Handles partial/unmounted lesson rooms to prevent them from looking broken or prematurely abandoned. Labeled as a calm room note.
- **Visual Priority**: Subdued fallback. Must float gracefully beneath main content.
- **Must Never Become**: A teaser module, a "coming soon" hype box, or a broken missing widget.

### 3. `StudentSourcePackReference`
- **Role**: Firmly anchors the folio to its external, rigor-layer Verification tracking document.
- **Visual Priority**: Tertirary metadata (often in `compact` sidebar variants). 
- **Must Never Become**: A vibrant call to action (CTA) button or download prompt.

### 4. `TransmissionSchematic`
- **Role**: Map indicating local structural chronology/dependencies between documents and arrangements. Uses flat, schematic blueprints.
- **Visual Priority**: Supporting graphic. Background and node strokes must stay below `25%` opacity or low-contrast gold tints.
- **Must Never Become**: A conspiracy board, proof schematic, or multi-directional mystery map.

### 5. `InteractiveGrid`
- **Role**: Typographical representation of unresolved, missing, or structural matrices. Handles explicit epistemic mapping for cells (`missing`, `reconstructed`, `disputed`).
- **Visual Priority**: Structural artifact fragment. Opacities hover around `80%`.
- **Must Never Become**: A clickable codebreaker grid, a solver mini-game, or a surface suggesting hidden decryption affordances. 

### 6. `VersionToggle`
- **Role**: Allows neutral lateral swapping between a historical primary witness structure and later interpreted reconstruction structures.
- **Visual Priority**: Flat utility switch. Selected states are bounded to `bg-parchment/40` to avoid dominating the surrounding content.
- **Must Never Become**: An adversarial "Correct vs Incorrect" judgment scale, or suggest progression/resolution.

### 7. `SourceComparisonLayout`
- **Role**: Presents comparative structural panels in parallel without preferring one over the other. 
- **Visual Priority**: Baseline container. Boundaries do not exceed `border-gold-dim/20`.
- **Must Never Become**: A high-contrast split view declaring one side complete and the other broken.
