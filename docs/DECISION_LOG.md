## [2026-04-15] Expand from 8 Pillars to 10

**Gate Level**: 4 (Co-Create)
**Proposed By**: Claude (based on Human's brief)
**Context**: The v1 architecture defined 8 pillars. The new brief introduced two critical capabilities: the Intelligence Observatory (contemplating what "intelligence" means) and the Research Lens (a toggleable critical-thinking overlay).
**Options Considered**:
  1. Keep 8 pillars, embed new capabilities in existing pillars — Pro: simpler. Con: buries the most important differentiators
  2. Expand to 10 pillars — Pro: gives Discernment and Intelligence their own space. Con: more to build
  3. Expand to 12 pillars (adding Journal and Profile as pillars) — Con: over-fragmentation
**Decision**: Expand to 10 pillars. The Research Lens and Intelligence Observatory are architecturally distinct enough to warrant their own identity.
**Rationale**: These two capabilities are what make this app different from every other occult reference. They deserve to be visible in the navigation, not buried inside other pillars.
**Impact**: SITE_MAP.md, PRODUCT_VISION.md, FEATURE_LIST.md all need v2 updates
**Decided By**: Human
**Status**: Confirmed (2026-04-15)

## [2026-04-15] Propose Revised Rank Sequence

**Gate Level**: 4 (Co-Create)
**Proposed By**: Claude
**Context**: The v1 ranks were Seeker → Student → Explorer → Adept → Archivist → Celestial Cartographer → Magus. The new brief emphasizes discernment over accumulation and anti-ego-inflation.
**Options Considered**:
  1. Keep v1 ranks — Pro: already documented. Con: "Magus" implies spiritual authority; no explicit discernment stage
  2. Revised sequence: Seeker → Student → Observer → Interpreter → Cartographer → Adept → Archivist — Pro: introduces observation and interpretation as explicit skills; final rank is stewardship, not mastery. Con: "Magus" is evocative and some users will miss it
  3. Hybrid: Keep Magus but add Observer/Interpreter — Con: 9 ranks may be too many
**Decision**: Proposed option 2. Awaiting Human confirmation.
**Rationale**: The app should not produce "magi." It should produce careful, discerning archivists of knowledge. The highest aspiration is stewardship, not personal spiritual authority.
**Impact**: PROGRESSION_SYSTEM.md needs full rewrite; curriculum restructured; UI profile system updated
**Decided By**: Human
**Status**: Confirmed (2026-04-15) — "Magus" removed. Final rank is Archivist. Stewardship over self-aggrandizement.
